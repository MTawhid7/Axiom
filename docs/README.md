# Axiom — Design Documentation

Axiom is a from-first-principles browser engine for personal use on macOS. **The project is in Phase Zero: research, requirements and architecture.**
- **Phase Zero's baseline is approved** (Step 6's Gate C, 2026-10-20): every ADR is accepted, and the architecture proposal, the roadmap and Milestone 1's specification are the baseline. **Now:** an independent, multi-round review of that baseline, on branch `architecture-review`, starting from the [handover](handover-phase-0.md). **Round 1** is written and disposed ([the review](review/README.md), 2026-10-21): the owner accepted all 22 findings; the changes are made, and spikes S10, S12 and S15 are done, their results approved. **Round 2** is delivered and disposed (2026-10-21): Round 1's changes verified, and 15 findings, none Critical or High, so the stopping rule is met for the scope commissioned; the owner accepted all 15, and their changes are made and verified. **Round 3,** an external review by Gemini, was delivered on 2026-10-21. **Round 4** (2026-10-22), commissioned by the owner, tested Round 3's claims, applied the owner's themes of scope by value, reuse and efficiency, reviewed every area no round had, and ran spike S16: 17 findings, 1 Critical and 1 High; the owner accepted all 17 the same day, and their changes are made and verified in checkpoints CP1–CP7. **The review cycle is complete** (the owner, 2026-10-22); `architecture-review` merged into `main` on 2026-10-22. **Milestone 1 started on 2026-10-23,** on branch `milestone-1`. At the owner's request for the discipline of "a mature software engineering organization", [the plan](milestone-1-plan.md) was revised with an engineering foundation, a walking skeleton and observability, and three documents written with ADRs 0022 and 0023: the [crate architecture](architecture/crates.md), the [engineering standards](technology/engineering-standards.md) and [observability](architecture/observability.md). The owner accepted both ADRs, adopted the plan as M1-CP1 and authorized MA10. **Now: M1-CP2,** the engineering foundation. The completed spikes (S1–S10, S12, S15 and S16) remain in `spikes/` as throwaway code; S11, S13 and S14 are authorized before M3.
- **Authorized:** Milestone 1's code, as [its plan](milestone-1-plan.md) stages it and MA10 lists it (the owner, 2026-10-23), with the owner's gates at the interface notes (M1-CP3, CP4, CP5, CP11), each dependency's adoption, any amendment, and the gate.

These documents are the project's source of truth. Conversation history is not.

## Conventions

- Dates are absolute (YYYY-MM-DD).
- Claims are labelled as sourced fact, inference, hypothesis or background ([research conventions](research/README.md)).
- Implemented behaviour is kept distinct from planned behaviour, and standards compliance from approximation.
- Every dependency carries a justification and is classed as one of:
  - a required platform facility;
  - a replaceable implementation detail;
  - a foundational dependency.
