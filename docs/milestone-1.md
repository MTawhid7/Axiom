## Summary

- **Milestone 1 is the static-document engine at L0** (MA1): HTML and the DOM without script, with XML documents (Round 4, RV4-01), style, CSS 2.1's visual formatting model without paged media, text with system fonts, PNG, JPEG, GIF and WebP, paint and the Metal compositor, an AppKit shell, and HTTP/1.1 over rustls; HTTP/2 moved to M2 (Round 4, RV4-08). **The browser with sandboxed renderer and network processes,** under S4's, S5's and S7's profiles, with every interface of the later levels (MS4; RV1-07); the compositor runs as a browser thread until M2 builds the GPU/compositor process (RV1-02).
- **It leaves out** script, cookies, storage, web fonts, SVG, flexbox, grid and the GPU/compositor process (MA2), and since Round 4 (RV4-08) HTTP/2, text entry into pages and the preload scanner.
- **Its specifications and suites are pinned at its start** (MA3): candidates today are WPT `0f26972422` and html5lib-tests `c777c408`.
- **Conformance without script** (MA4), because WPT's testharness tests are script:
  - html5lib's tokenizer tests: 100%;
  - the 1,973 tree-construction tests: at least 96%, the shipping median rounded down (SB11);
  - `css/CSS2`'s reference tests in scope that need no script, 5,652 of 5,848: **at least 90.0%, and no directory of 100 tests or more over 10 points below its shipping median** (Round 4, RV4-09). 5,423 of them are XML documents or reach one, which is why M1 builds the XML parser (RV4-01). **An interim target** (SB11): the full shipping median, over all 5,848 with the 196 tests that need script, applies at M3, with WebDriver (RV1-01, RV4-09);
  - Unicode's line-breaking and bidi conformance files: 100%.
- **The harness** (MA4, MA11): wptrunner's `axiom` product with a script-free reference-test executor, one process per test, as wptrunner's process-per-test Servo executor works; run end to end at the second checkpoint (RV1-01).
- **The corpus** (MA5): Wikipedia's article on π and MDN's canvas tutorial render readably from dated local copies and live, links navigate, and 10 loads of each never crash or hang.
- **Budgets** (MA6): the browser idle with one window at most 85.4 MB, a ready renderer at most 10 MB, the network process at most 25 MB; Wikipedia's page at most 100 MB and MDN's at most 70 MB above it; S6's frame budgets; no task over 50 ms; no idle wakeups; pauses within 4 ms; T1–T4.
- **Security and robustness** (MA7): no script engine linked; no credentials, by construction; every parser and decoder fuzzed for at least an hour under AddressSanitizer with no open finding; `unsafe` only where the policy allows; every dependency registered and graded; every executable aborts on a panic, and validators are panic-free with checked arithmetic (RV1-16).
- **Diagnostics** (MA1): the headless embedder dumps the DOM, computed styles, the box and fragment trees, the display list and the network log as text (RV1-15).
- **Determinism** (MA8): parsing and painting independent of chunking and repetition.
- **Starting it needs the owner's authorization** (MA10): the branch, the skeleton under `crates/`, the dependencies listed, the test data, and the tools, including a nightly chosen with spike S12's result for ThreadSanitizer (RV1-11).

## 7. Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The milestone is large:** the whole static pipeline | High | A long milestone | Checkpoints that each leave the workspace working (MA11); the gate counts only M1's scope |
| **The CSS2 median is high,** set by mature engines | High | The conformance gate is missed | M1's target is already interim, over the 5,652 tests that need no script (RV1-01). Neither new engine meets it yet on those tests: Servo 93.7%, Ladybird 91.4% (measured from wpt.fyi's runs; Ladybird's at its own revision). A miss with a reason goes to the owner (SB11) |
| **The script-free executor and WebDriver's disagree** on some tests | Low | M3's results shift when WebDriver arrives | Both compare screenshots under the tests' fuzzy tolerances. At M3 the selection runs under both before WebDriver replaces the script-free executor, and any difference is explained |
| **ThreadSanitizer stays broken** on macOS 27 for Rust's nightlies | Medium | `platform`'s threads lose one check | TS11's first three checks don't depend on it (RV1-11); spike S12 tries two routes before M1; recorded as blocked otherwise (MA7), and rechecked with each nightly |
| **Text differs from CoreText's rendering** (S5) | Medium | The readability check fails on details | S5's measured differences are known; the judged check looks at readability, not pixel identity |
| **Wikipedia's chrome needs flexbox and grid** (S8) | High | The page's header and navigation look wrong | The criteria judge the article's content (MA5); the chrome is M4's |
| **A helper's profile silently denies something M1's code needs** (as S4, S5 and S7 found) | Medium | A feature fails only inside the sandbox | S5's and S7's metadata rules carried over; the probes in each helper; the system log's denials (`/usr/bin/log`); single-process mode to tell engine bugs from denials (RV1-07) |
